### Overview
An intelligent triage architecture developed for the Global Innovation Challenge to help citizens assess legal risks and connect with appropriate legal resources.

### Technologies Used
- **AI Model:** Gemini 3.1 Pro
- **Framework:** Google Antigravity
- **Backend:** FastAPI (Python)
- **Architecture:** Agentic Framework

### Key Features
✅ Advanced NLP for legal query parsing  
✅ Intelligent routing to appropriate legal resources  
✅ Multi-language support  
✅ User-friendly interface  
✅ Scalable architecture  

### Achievements
- Successfully demonstrated at Global Innovation Challenge
- Built end-to-end solution from concept to deployment
- Implemented advanced LLM integration
- Focus on accessibility for underserved communities

### Impact
Democratizing access to legal guidance and resources for citizens who may not have access to traditional legal services.

## 🚜 Multimodal Farmer Query Support System

### Overview
An innovative AI-based support system designed to bridge the digital divide by providing agricultural guidance in local dialects, making modern farming knowledge accessible to rural communities.

### Technologies Used
- **AI Framework:** Multimodal Intelligence Frameworks
- **NLP:** Natural Language Processing
- **Local Dialect Processing:** Regional language support
- **Backend:** FastAPI / Python
- **Frontend:** Mobile-optimized web interface

### Key Features
✅ Local dialect understanding and response  
✅ Agricultural query analysis  
✅ Real-time farming recommendations  
✅ Weather and crop-specific guidance  
✅ Offline functionality support  
✅ Accessibility-first design  

### Problem Addressed
- Language barrier for rural farmers
- Limited access to modern agricultural techniques
- Digital divide between urban and rural areas
- Difficulty in obtaining timely farming advice

### Solution Highlights
- Implemented multimodal AI for context understanding
- Trained on regional agricultural knowledge
- User-friendly interface for non-tech-savvy users
- Provides recommendations in local languages

### Impact
Empowering rural farmers with:
- Improved crop yields through modern techniques
- Better resource management
- Climate adaptation strategies
- Market information and pricing
- Real-time pest and disease management
